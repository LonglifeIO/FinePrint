How a family safety app shares driving data

Our services help families stay connected wherever they are, every day of the week, at home or away.

We use the location of your phone to show your circle where you are, and to warn you before you arrive somewhere new.

We share driving data with insurers for risk scoring.

Insurers may use that data to set the price of your policy.

You can turn off Drive Detection in the app's settings at any time, and your earlier trips stay in your history.

Questions about this policy go to our privacy team, who answer within thirty days of receiving them.
